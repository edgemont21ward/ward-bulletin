How Preview and Publish work

Preview shows how the tab you're on will look, without publishing anything. It turns yellow when that tab isn't what's live: a different tab, or this one edited since it was published.

Publish makes the tab you're on the live bulletin everyone sees, and shows it to you when it's done.
